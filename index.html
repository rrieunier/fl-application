The actions

Listen first, then act, along five axes:

  • Talent Retention
  • Faction Expertise
  • Business Development
  • Operational Excellence
  • Talent Acquisition
AXIS 1

Talent Acquisition

Flagship: AI-native junior pipeline. Recruit juniors who started their careers with AI tooling; pair each with a senior in a two-way exchange.
  • Redesign buddying as reverse-mentoring: AI workflows ↔ domain & technical knowledge
  • Referral loop: consultants become the first recruiting channel
Impact: faster ramp-up, fresh practices, a balanced seniority pyramid.
AXIS 2

Talent Retention

Flagship: listen, anticipate, grow. Consultant survey first, then act only on what people really want.
  • Know contract terms to anticipate requests (raises, evolutions, mobility) before they become resignations
  • Regular 1:1s focused on ambitions and a visible growth path
  • Unofficial roles for mediors/seniors = ownership and career signal
Impact: lower attrition, higher engagement, no wasted effort.
AXIS 3

Business Development

Flagship: faction as a pre-sales asset. Skill map of the faction, ready for staffing requests, RFIs and RFPs.
  • Reusable offer & reference sheets built from consultants' missions
  • Consultants invited into client conversations and solution design
  • Close the loop with sales on pipeline vs. skills to develop
Impact: faster, sharper proposals; better staffing fit; growth aligned with the market.
AXIS 4

Operational Excellence

Flagship: workload & staffing cockpit. Use Faction Lead meetings to balance load and match skills to ambitions.
  • Shared view: availability, upcoming ends of mission, ambitions
  • Delegate initiatives (ambition follow-up, internal workshops, knowledge manager link) to mediors/seniors
  • Light rituals: fewer, better follow-ups
Impact: less bench time and overload, my time freed for leadership and delivery.
AXIS 5

Faction Expertise

Flagship: the learning loop. Juniors and mediors regularly present a tech or method — 15 minutes, turning into discussion.
  • Topics driven by the strategy: Databricks, Snowflake, Terraform, Airflow, K8s, AI-assisted engineering
  • Presentation skills grow as a by-product
  • Field experience fed back into the offering
Impact: shared technical level, visible expertise, stronger offering.
